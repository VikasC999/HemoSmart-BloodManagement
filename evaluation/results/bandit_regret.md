Regret = donations the oracle got minus donations the policy got, cumulative to that day (lower is better). The oracle ranks by true rate every day; with eligibility gaps that is a strong reference, not a proven optimum, so slightly negative regret is possible.

Hidden response rates are drawn from Beta(2, 2) by `simulate_donors()`; real donor behaviour (time of day, distance, fatigue from repeated alerts) isn't modelled.
