# HemoSmart — Synthetic Patient Data vs Real Data

Generated by `python -m evaluation.data_realism` on 2026-10-30 22:35. Synthetic: `data/patient_data.csv` (5,000 patients). Real: NHANES 2021–23 CBC_L + DEMO_L, adults 18+ (6,004 people), **weighted with the MEC exam weights (WTMEC2YR)**. Sources and generator parameters: [data/DATA_CARD.md](../../data/DATA_CARD.md).

**Read the differences with the target in mind.** NHANES is the US general population. HemoSmart simulates Indian surgical patients, so lower hemoglobin and more anaemia are intended (NFHS-5: anaemia in 25% of men, 57% of women; ISOS: 30% pre-operative anaemia), as is a thrombocytopenic tail. A small KS distance would be the wrong goal here.

## Not checked here

- **INR:** NHANES has no coagulation tests. The generator's ~4% above 1.5 is an estimate (no clean published pre-operative figure found).
- **Transfusion rates by surgery:** no open patient-level dataset; they are calibrated to published rates (TRICS III, NSQIP, Indian MSBOS audit, level-I trauma) — see the data card.
- **Age:** uniform 18–89 in the generator; not compared.
