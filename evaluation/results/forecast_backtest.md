Coverage = share of actual days that fell inside the band. Near 80% means the band can be read as stated; well below means it is too narrow.

MAE in units/day (lower is better). Bias = mean(forecast − actual): negative means under-forecasting, which for blood means a late donor call. The noise floor is the error of the true expected demand — Poisson variation no forecast can remove.
