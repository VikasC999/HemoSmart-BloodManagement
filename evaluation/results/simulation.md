A real shortage counts as projected L days earlier if the cycle L or more days before it listed that group/component as short with a first shortage day on or before it, within the 7-day horizon. Lead 0 is the same morning's cycle, a few hours before demand. Precision understates the Planner: every projected shortage triggers donor outreach, so a warning that worked (donors came, the shortage never happened) counts here as a false alarm. The Planner projects expected (mean) demand, so a shortage caused by a one-day demand spike above the forecast is not projected at all; planning on an upper quantile catches more of them at a cost in wastage (`--planning-sweep`, docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md D81).

Policies: **baseline** = manual (own-group FIFO, reactive random donor calls); **allocation** = + wastage-aware allocation; **agents** = + autonomous forecast/gap/bandit loop; **hemosmart** = + redistribution to the partner hospital. Redistributed units are reported separately, not counted as used.

Simulated, not measured: donor replies (hidden per-donor rates, mean ~23%), the partner's acceptance, and routine supply (Poisson around the Planner's assumed rate, with an April-May dip that this August-October window doesn't reach). See docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md D83.
