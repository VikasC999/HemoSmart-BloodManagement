# HemoSmart — Donor Response on Real Data

UCI Blood Transfusion Service Center dataset (Yeh et al. 2009), 748 real donors. Outcome: donated in March 2007. 5-fold stratified cross-validation, mean ± std across folds. Generated by `python -m evaluation.donor_real_data` on 2026-10-30 22:41.

## Ranking who donates next

| Method | AUROC | Precision, top 10% | Lift over base rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Random order | 0.548 ± 0.031 | 0.213 ± 0.027 | 0.896 ± 0.108 |
| Beta posterior mean (bandit idea) | 0.735 ± 0.046 | 0.533 ± 0.152 | 2.240 ± 0.636 |
| Logistic regression (R, F, T) | 0.753 ± 0.029 | 0.627 ± 0.090 | 2.632 ± 0.368 |

Beta prior from history: responses = Frequency, alerts = floor(Time / 3) + 1 donation opportunities (one per 90-day eligibility window), raised to Frequency for the 38 donors who gave more often than that. The dataset records donations, not alerts, so this mapping is an assumption. The posterior has no fitted parameters; the logistic regression is trained on each training fold. Monetary is exactly 250 c.c. × Frequency, so it is left out.

The posterior ignores Recency (months since the last donation), which the logistic regression uses; how much that matters is the gap between the two rows.

## Response rate: real vs simulated

Real donors: **23.8%** donated in the target month. The posterior means from the mapped history average 47.3%, about twice that: past giving overstates future response, mostly because many donors have lapsed (high Recency).

The simulator used to draw each donor's hidden reply rate from Beta(2, 2), mean 50%. Because of this result it now draws from Beta(1.5, 5.0), mean **23.1%** (-0.7 points from the real rate; docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md D83). That match is by construction, not a validation, and a month's donation and a reply to one alert are not the same quantity: the simulated rate is an anchored assumption.

The random row is one random ordering per fold, so it scatters around AUROC 0.5 and lift 1.
